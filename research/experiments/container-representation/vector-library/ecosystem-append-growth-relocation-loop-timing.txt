Purpose: reproduce the registered concrete scalar/wide relocation trial; retain
until superseded by qualified relocation lowering. Sole API: Vector append.
Dependencies: ecosystem-append-growth-full-route-timing.txt and its replay patch
reconstruct exact B. Earlier compiler overlays/frozen CLI remain prerequisites;
this record does not substitute production compiler output. No production,
specification, source-acceptance, representation or allocator policy is selected.

Only B's two full-positive memcpy calls in each module change to an internal
always-inline helper, with guarded 64-byte chunks then guarded 8-byte words.
Each chunk loads four 16-byte vectors before storing them. Access alignment is 1;
there are no new alias promises, volatile accesses or optimizer barriers in the
candidate. Concrete inputs are i64 and {[32 x i64]}: integral words without
padding or pointers, byte count divisible by 8, not necessarily 64. Pointer-bearing
relocation is not qualified. Allocation, NULL refusal, exact copy extent, old
payload free, descriptor publication, empty and partial paths remain as B.
