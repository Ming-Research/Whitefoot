Growth-body, growth-chain and complete append exposure: frozen-Z diagnostics

Purpose: reproduce the specialization-versus-duplication trials registered in
RESULTS.md. These patches, samples and evidence retire with a superseding qualified
lowering result. No production inlining policy or representation is selected.

All three trials independently start from frozen empty-anchor Z, reproduced by
empty-payload-anchor.patch and its timing record. Body adds only two alwaysinline
definition tokens per timed/account module (scalar/wide wf_grow). Chain adds six
per module (wf_grow, grow_full and reserve at both widths). The final spine trial
adds ten: that chain plus make_room and grow_vector_append at both widths.
Exact removal restores
each input byte for byte; replay also reproduces every output. No new assumptions,
source edits, allocator changes or history read. Public API entries and
resident-clone definitions receive no new attribute;
make_room is newly tagged only in the final ten-token trial.

Body fails its native prerequisite: allocation moves into reserve, but append
still calls reserve and the repeated full/partial test survives there. No timing.
Chain passes its revised native prerequisite: append calls make_room, whose single
full-window test reaches malloc/realloc without a second full/partial dispatch.
General partial reserve still has malloc/memmove/free; allocation null failures
precede payload/capacity publication. All three trials' 19 stages have expected exits;
all 30 accounting rows are byte-identical to Z. No failed check was retried.
