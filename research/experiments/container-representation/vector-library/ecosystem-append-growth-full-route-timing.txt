Full-positive growth route: paired attribution record

Purpose: reproduce the registered Vector append allocation/movement comparison;
retire with a qualified growth implementation. This is research on the existing
nonconforming inline-owner/static-anchor overlay. No production compiler/library/
driver change, specification revision or route policy is adopted. Other APIs pause.

A is the frozen complete-append exposure image from the prior exposure record.
B starts with its exact two LLVM modules and replaces only each scalar/wide
full-positive realloc block with malloc, a null check, initialized-prefix memcpy,
old-payload free and the existing descriptor publication. Empty/partial/ZST paths
and all ten exposure attributes remain. No extra alignment/alias facts are added.
This compares complete routes and their generated code, not allocator internals.
B's API account-only flag expects malloc/free instead of realloc in eight positive
WF rows. All 30 rows otherwise preserve requests, byte extents, capacity and final
live bytes; zero-capacity and peer rows are unchanged. Simultaneous live payload
is old plus new. Timed driver object, peers, runtime and link order are unchanged.
The copied raw LLVM inputs are intentionally A's modules, not Z's earlier modules.

Native: public entries still tail to reached instances. Full-positive append calls
malloc, checks null, calls memcpy for len*stride, frees old payload and publishes.
The same spare/full and capacity-policy branches remain. No new internal growth
helper or q-register payload spill appears; wide construction follows allocation.
Frames grow from 48 to 80 bytes for register preservation; reached scalar/wide
bodies grow 43/107 to 59/123 instructions. Total WF instruction bytes grow 17860 to
18996 (+1136), counting unique AArch64 instruction addresses including alignment
nops, excluding data/other objects. Partial reserve retains malloc/memmove/free.
Code/register/copy-call changes belong to this route comparison's measured cost.

Prerequisites: all 20 API/account stages and 24 focused stages return their expected
exits, independently cross-checked against raw guard END lines. Focused check and
emission initially pass, without fixture repair. The observer's fixed expectations
cover scalar and nested Box<u64> full relocation, ordered element cleanup, partial
spare bytes, empty allocation, ZST, ranges, linked owner transfer and foreign
anchors. Real NULL injection at the newly introduced full malloc exits 73 only
after observing all descriptor words, initialized contents and nested owners
unchanged, with no copy/free/publication; first-allocation refusal exits 74.
Early-free and early-descriptor-write IR mutants fail for scalar and owning cases;
wrong-copy-order swaps two valid 8-byte elements and fails before old retirement.
Linked checks replace ordinary public LLVM bodies; they do not qualify source
fn_sig formation. The API extra-allocation counter fault is not an OOM witness.

Instrument audit: initial timed preprocessor byte comparison differed only in
Darwin assert's embedded input basename. Feeding both source byte streams to the
same stdin filename yields identical output; the actual timed driver object is
unchanged throughout. This was no compiler/test rejection or timing rerun.
