Spare append A/B cross-check: frozen full-growth-route images

Purpose: qualify the spare path of this append API under the registered RAW-clock
screen. Retire with final append qualification. No source, compiler, driver or
new replay patch. Dependencies, source/observer qualification and unchanged timed
objects are those in ecosystem-append-growth-full-route-timing.txt and
full-growth-route.patch; no additional compiler capability is claimed.

A is complete append exposure; B replaces its full-positive growth route with
malloc/memcpy/free. Images are frozen as below. Existing timed/account api-check
passes in the dependency record include zero allocations during spare append.
The two new growth-api-check calls additionally verify each image's RAW clock:
200000 reads, 41 ns minimum, quantized=0, exit 0. Then one fixed A1-B1-B2-A2
sequence runs api-measure 4194304 7 with ordinary preparation and empty-batch
controls. All four screen exits are 0. No rebuild, retry, filtering or subtraction.

Native audit: read otool -tvV of both frozen images, following public
wf_vector_api_{word,record}_append_batch tail entries to their ordinary instances.
Scalar A/B frame 80 B; same eight-instruction spare loop modulo register allocation,
capacity retained in a register and final length stored at batch exit. Wider
cold growth bodies differ but are not taken by this pre-reserved workload.
Wide A/B frames 336/352 B. Each stores seven q-register pairs (224B constants)
once before entering the loop; reloads occur after growth calls, not per appended
value on the spare path. B has extra entry preservation and a 16 B larger frame.
Rust scalar/wide frames 48/304 B; wide also saves 224 B constants before its loop.
C++ scalar frame 112 B; wide batch frame 64 B calls a 64 B emplace_back helper for every
record. The helper constructs directly into spare storage; the call is not itself
evidence of a bulk payload copy. These native observations do not assign elapsed
time shares to instructions, setup stores or call boundaries.
