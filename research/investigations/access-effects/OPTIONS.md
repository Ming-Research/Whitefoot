# Option catalog for the ownership redesign

Research date: 2026-09-16. This catalog lists every option enumerated for the
decision points of the requirement-first ownership redesign, merged from five
enumeration lenses (theory, systems practice, AI writer, radical, Whitefoot
history) recorded in EVIDENCE-option-enumeration-2026-09-16.md (`EVIDENCE-option-enumeration-2026-09-16.md`, removed in the cleanup, recoverable at commit 4ca62f8db758).
Nothing here is ranked, scored or pruned; outlandish options are kept and
marked. The debates that select among them record their verdicts and every
refused option with its reason in VERDICTS.md when they exist.
Supersede in place.

Decision points are those of [MECHANISM-MAP.md](MECHANISM-MAP.md) section 6
to 8, restated here as questions; missing decision points the lenses found are
appended.

Row counts: 1472 option rows from five lenses; every row ID appears exactly once in the Members column of one family below; a few IDs are cited again inside a tensions cell to mark a contradiction between members, and a script confirms none is missing.

## D2 Where permission and state live

Axes: (1) carrier — checker-internal context, source text (type, mode, convention, effect, token, capability value), a separate artifact (certificate, sidecar, lock file, schedule), or run time (tag, hardware bit, allocator/OS metadata, log); (2) who writes it — nothing written, writer-authored, compiler-generated then transcribed or reviewed, or inferred whole-program; (3) locality of the fact — per statement/call site, per function signature, per object, per thread/ambient, or global; (4) erasure — fully erased before lowering, erased in release only, or retained at run time; (5) whether the permission is a value the program threads (linear token, capability, guard) or a judgment about the program (context grade, type index, effect row, analysis result).

## D5 The source of interference facts for parallel permission and the optimizer

Axes: (1) what the fact is about — storage identities/footprints, references (loans), regions or index domains, time (phases), values (monoids, lattices), or program structure (schedule, channels, spawn tree); (2) who supplies it — writer-written, derived from bodies, pinned from a printed derivation, or discovered by a runtime; (3) when it is discharged — at compile time under a fixed decidable family, at schedule-build time, or dynamically with static summaries; (4) which guarantee the permission promises — source-order equality, confluence, observational determinism, race freedom, invariant preservation, or equality modulo a declared operator; (5) how the fact reaches the optimizer — as a per-span or per-declaration alias promise, as loop metadata, or not at all.

## D8 Storage placement, relocation and moves

Axes: (1) whether storage may relocate at all — never by construction, only at an explicit source event, or freely with a fixup channel; (2) who decides placement — the writer per binding, a contract, the compiler by inference, a separate schedule or stage, or the allocator; (3) how interior identity survives — index handles, scoped projections, pins or immobility marks, or re-derivation after a name change; (4) the granularity of the storage-ending event — a single owner, an inline slot, a multi-target commit, a whole arena, or the whole store; (5) whether the option is a source-language rule or a lowering/backend fact.

## D9 Container elements and backings
