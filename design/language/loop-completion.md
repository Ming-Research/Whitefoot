Decision: An ordinary loop retains a normal successor exactly when a break resolves to its structural identity, and a final loop without that successor satisfies value-initializer delivery by divergence, because a return-only loop or an endless iteration cannot fall through and requiring a trailing return invents a source path with no execution, instead of giving every ordinary loop a conservative fallthrough edge; counted loops retain their false-header edge, and an initializer still requires a nonempty give delivery set.
