Decision: A place written through a reference variable is identified, as a term and as a Goal datum, by the one path that variable names where the variable is exact, and by its spelling through the variable otherwise; a variable is exact when no `set` of its body rebinds it and its path is exact, and a path is exact when it has no descendant cover, every index and range step holds its own formation's value, and it starts at owned or constant storage or at a parameter or `atomic` binder that no `set` rebinds, because a guard read through a payload binder or a local alias and the requirement of a call through it are instantiated over the same storage and must meet whichever name reaches it, which `fn8-pos-payload-guard-append-grow` and `fn8-pos-guard-through-local-alias` exercise, while a variable or root whose one declaration names different storage before and after a rebinding would let a fact about one place discharge a requirement about another, as the resolved referent of an actual formed from a later-rebound parameter or `atomic` binder did against a guard spelled through that variable (`fn8-neg-guard-through-rebound-parameter`, `fn8-neg-guard-through-rebound-atomic-binder`), instead of identifying every place by its canonical spelling alone.

Decision: Direct segment and page selections compare their proof-path selectors as index steps, while a borrowed page keeps its formation identity, because segment boundaries are fixed and a direct page selection observes the current initialized extent on each evaluation, with writes to the owner's length or the selector's support killing its facts, while a borrowed page captures an extent that survives later append, instead of giving direct page reads fresh identities or identifying borrowed extents across formations.

Rejected:
- Spelling-only identity for every place: rejected because a guard read through a reference variable proves nothing about a call through that variable once growth or an append loop kills the placement equalities connecting the two spellings.
- Judging exactness at each read regardless of rebindings: rejected because a variable rebound inside a loop then names its target through the exact path before the loop and through its spelling at the loop header, so a header invariant read through it can no longer be proved from the facts established on entry, which the spelling of a rebound variable at every point keeps connected.
- Entry referents for rebound parameters and `atomic` binders, with rebinding kills and capture-to-source associations: rejected because it adds identities and kill events whose only use is a path rooted at a rebound variable, which keeping such paths apart already makes sound.
