Decision: A range fact binds one or two variables and states guarded affine relations over literals, consts, bound variables, live integer values, measures, segment lengths and integer element reads, because the facts the level cascade and the scatter producers need are pointwise or pairwise, as a left inverse `positions[order[k]] == k` or `depth[parent[e]] + 1 == depth[e]`, and every further binder multiplies the instances a derivation forms ([derivation](../../../research/investigations/unique-keys/POINTWISE.md)), instead of arbitrary nesting or alternation of quantifiers.

Decision: A range fact is proved at every call of a function requiring it, at its loop's entry and on every backedge, by one fixed derivation: active facts are instantiated at the values the problem's own element reads select through the fact's reads, a read of a written version splits into the written element and the old one, a joined value into the arm taken, and two reads of one version into the same element and different ones, over solved equalities, read congruence and Fourier-Motzkin elimination tightened over the integers, because those are the steps a pointwise invariant's preservation takes after one element write, a frontier element's case and a certificate's separation, and each is bounded by what the problem already holds, so the judgment searches nothing and always terminates, instead of an SMT solver with quantifier instantiation or written instances for every obligation.

Decision: A derivation that overflows its checked `i128` arithmetic or reaches one of its structural ceilings, 4096 atoms, 256 instances of one fact, 4096 inequalities in one elimination or 4096 branches, rejects the site and names the ceiling, because acceptance must be fixed by the specification rather than by a work budget the machine sets, as the 4096 `proof_use` ceiling already is; the numbers are provisional, chosen above what the measured programs reach, instead of an unbounded derivation or an unproved verdict at the ceiling.

Decision: Range facts cross a call only as the callee's requirements, so a consumer establishes them over storage it receives by a validating loop whose invariants state them, and live storage carries no range fact across passes, because a renderer's document is changed by scripts between passes, so a fact kept with it would be owed again by every mutation while a pass already walks what it styles in O(n) and can check the structure it reads ([plan](../../../research/investigations/unique-keys/POINTWISE.md#the-architecture-rule-each-pass-derives-its-facts)), instead of range postconditions or facts maintained by every document mutation; reopen when one producer must hand facts to several consumers that cannot afford a validating pass.

Rejected:
- Three or more bound variables, such as distinctness within each segment of a `Segments` as `(d, a, b)`: rejected because a left inverse over two variables states the same distinctness, `positions[s[d][k]] == k`, at a fraction of the instances.
- Written instances for every obligation, as a certificate gives a loop: rejected because a preservation proof would then restate, at every write, instances the problem's own reads already select.
