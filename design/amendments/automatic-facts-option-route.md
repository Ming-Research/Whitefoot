Node: language/checks-and-proofs/automatic-facts

Decision: A local own Result or Option with an integer payload carries conditional evidence for its current success payload, `Ok` or `Some`, through ordinary binding, copying, moving, replacement and value delivery, its failure variant `Err` or `None` contributing a contradictory success context, and success selection or a forwarded-return judgment reads that evidence under its guard, because the matched examples in research/investigations/result-proof-transport/DESIGN.md lose the same verified relation merely by naming or propagating its value, and an Option is the same two-variant shape as a Result with a unit error, which writers adopted only to keep a payload bound, as the [writer-lost-facts probes](../../research/investigations/writer-lost-facts/DESIGN.md#shape-4-option-results) show, instead of a closed set of direct-call source shapes that forces writers to reconstruct equivalent values, or Result-only evidence that forces the unit-error detour. This replaces the node's decision beginning "A local own Result with an integer payload carries conditional evidence", which it extends to Option unchanged otherwise. The owner approved this revision to land together with the struct payload routes of PR #169; the carrier-equality decision proposed beside it has entered the tree.

Rejected:
- A success route for every enum with one integer-payload variant: rejected because Option is the one reported consumer and, like Result, has one fixed success and one fixed failure variant.
