Decision: Recommend withdrawing the proposal to supply an unsigned entry-length-below-capacity fact at every Ring back placement, because the [complete Deque comparison](../../research/experiments/container-representation/deque-library/RESULTS.md#entry-capacity-pair-scalar-gains-wide-regression-candidate-rejected) finds three qualified scalar growth gains but a qualified wide growth regression that violates the recorded selection rule, instead of adopting a valid fact whose uniform lowering fails that performance requirement. The proposal formerly replaced the existing address-fact decision's clause "Logical window coordinates and wrapping arithmetic receive no additional assertion" with "Other logical window coordinates and wrapping arithmetic receive no additional assertion"; the recommended disposition leaves the live decision unchanged. The experiment is rejected and its compiler patch is retained as frozen evidence. This amendment records the recommended withdrawal pending owner disposition, not an owner ruling or a new live-tree decision.

Decision: Propose limiting the unsigned Ring back-placement entry-capacity fact to elements using the existing ordinary SSA-value representation, withholding it for stored aggregates, because the [complete representation-limited comparison](../../research/experiments/container-representation/deque-library/RESULTS.md#representation-limited-pair-scalar-gains-historical-target-unresolved) preserves aggregate control instructions and obtains both required scalar growth gains without a qualified loss or lost contemporaneous target pass, instead of uniform emission or fixture-specific exceptions. Selection remains pending under the unchanged criterion: a historical scalar forward-churn target pass disappears in both current arms, leaving its historical-target condition unresolved without establishing a candidate-caused regression. The proposal would replace the live decision's clause "Logical window coordinates and wrapping arithmetic receive no additional assertion" with "Ring back placement of an element in the ordinary SSA-value representation receives the unsigned entry-length-below-capacity relation from its discharged entry precondition; other logical window coordinates and wrapping arithmetic receive no additional assertion." The existing direct-store versus stored-payload transfer boundary is coarse: one-word structs and arrays are excluded, post-inlining representations may coincide, and it proves neither profitability nor completion of the standard-peer target. Reopen for an independently specified final-current-main comparison, a representation-classification change or a native-consumer change; the original criterion and uniform trial's refusal remain in force, and no live-tree ruling is inferred.

Rejected:
- Selecting the tested uniform entry-capacity fact: rejected because the experiment finds that 256-byte growth at count 4096 regresses with complete observed-range separation in both cohorts; the scalar improvement does not override the registered no-regression condition.
- Supplying the new fact to every window operation or every wrap helper call: rejected because this mapping is the entry precondition of Ring back placement; a front-removal offset can equal capacity, and Slots back placement already addresses its logical offset directly without a capacity load.
- Comparing the logical quantities as signed integers or asserting that head plus length is below capacity: rejected because admitted zero-size storage can have capacity above the signed range and an admitted nonzero-head back placement can cross the physical end.
- Adding arithmetic flags, a head-zero assertion, a source operation or a bulk transfer with this fact: rejected because each has a different proof or performance question and would prevent the recorded comparison from isolating the entry-capacity fact.
- Choosing the fact by fixture identity, source type name or a payload-byte threshold: rejected because the current question concerns an existing transfer-representation distinction and those alternatives introduce a different policy without independent evidence.
- Treating the representation predicate as a profitability theorem: rejected because small stored aggregates can lower like scalars after inlining, and direct values can also acquire costly vectorization; the native and complete-family discriminators must decide the provisional policy.
