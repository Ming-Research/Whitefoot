Node: compiler/parallel-lowering/two-worlds

Decision: Under --par, each ordinary cyclic call-graph component gets one budget variant per member behind its ordinary signature and result ABI; each call entering the component obtains its initial budget once, a call into the component that belongs to an actualized statement group (a published member's callback, the inline member and its refused edge) decrements it, a call into the component outside every group passes it on unchanged, and exhaustion enters the sequential clone, because the budget bounds how deeply offers nest and a call outside every group offers nothing: spending a level there exhausted the default budget of about eight levels on the one-child descents of a deep document before its wide sibling runs, holding Snowghost's preorder style traversal to 1.04 times at four workers on ecma262 and 2.57 on html5, while spending only at groups reaches 3.10 and 3.67 and changes one formal kernel's emission ([recursion budget at splits](../../../research/investigations/call-offer-grain/DESIGN.md#the-recursion-budget-at-splits)), and the [recursion-budget comparison](https://github.com/mbbill/Whitefoot/blob/b3341e32ab8d69e2974227aee7146ac16eb2fe00/research/investigations/compute-runtime/RESULTS.md#recursion-budget-comparison) supports a single compare and branch per node above the cut with none below, instead of N cloned parallel levels, a global nesting bound, or a decrement at every call into the component. Components with a synthesized loop, a waiting member or no sequential clone are excluded with a --par-ledger reason and make the offers they reach without a budget; this is offer selection after checking. A waiting member is excluded because it is a resumable frame entered by a transfer, with no ordinary signature for a variant to stand behind. This replaces the node's decision beginning "Under --par, each ordinary cyclic call-graph component gets one budget variant per member".

Rejected:
- Decrementing the budget at every call into the component: rejected because a recursion that descends through many calls outside every group spends its levels before it reaches a group, which held Snowghost's preorder style traversal to 1.04 times at four workers on ecma262 and apollo11.
- Keeping the decrement at every call and raising the default budget: rejected because the descent depth belongs to the input, so no fixed allowance covers every document, and a larger budget also deepens offer nesting in balanced recursions, where the depth and width comparison chose floor(log2(64 times the lane count)).
