Decision: Provisionally lower Slots insertion/removal suffix shifts as one overlapping transfer of complete target-stride representations for the matched [Vector trial](../../research/experiments/container-representation/vector-library/RESULTS.md#contiguous-slots-shift-trial-criterion-before-implementation), retaining Ring insertion/removal and the existing append/splitting walks for both shapes and retaining proved-empty release without element release, because OP-10 bounds the contiguous Slots extent and STOR-7 permits owner relocation, instead of requiring the shared per-element walk for this shape before testing its cost.
