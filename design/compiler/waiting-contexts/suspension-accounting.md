Decision: Account for each native context invocation with a monotonically increasing odd/even sequence on the physical executor's own cache line, covering resume through disposition publication, and confirm quiescence with two sequentially consistent collections of all 128 executor slots around the driver-state scan, because a displaced executor can still compute after its logical driver changes owners and an unchanged inactive generation excludes a complete invocation between the collections ([argument](../../../research/investigations/suspension-accounting/DESIGN.md#3-the-invocation-sequence-and-the-snapshot-argument)), instead of modifying process-wide counters on every cooperative suspension. The fixed slots match the runtime's executor ceiling and are never reset or reused during the process lifetime.

Decision: Serialize quiescence scans and cold idle, wake and transfer transitions with one native mutex, retain logical idleness through empty maintenance polls and reactivate before taking work, because failed probes and repeated empty polls can otherwise invalidate every attempted snapshot of a persistent deadlock, instead of relying on repeated optimistic scans alone; invocation boundaries remain outside this mutex, and the deadlock report's recheck takes it inside the context roster's lock because no section under it enters the roster ([soundness and liveness](../../../research/investigations/suspension-accounting/DESIGN.md#4-persistent-deadlock-and-the-cold-mutex)).

Decision: Publish a fairness-only suspension to its driver's ready queue after native unwind without using the external-wake gate, while retaining that gate for registered waits, because a fairness boundary registers no external waker and its executing invocation remains visible until queue or ingress publication, instead of using a wake-race handshake for a disposition with no competing publisher ([publication](../../../research/investigations/suspension-accounting/DESIGN.md#5-fair-suspension-and-pending-host-work)).

Rejected:
- A shared active-invocation count and a shared change increment on every native return: rejected because every cooperative switch on every driver then writes the same cache lines, a cost that grows with the number of drivers, while a per-executor sequence keeps those writes on one core's own line.
- One invocation slot per logical driver: rejected because a replacement can enter that driver while its displaced physical executor is still computing.
- One collection or an active Boolean alone: rejected because an invocation can begin and end between observations without changing the final inactive value.
- A double collect without serialized cold transitions and persistent idle state: rejected because failed probes and empty polls can continually invalidate scans although the program has no possible next step.
