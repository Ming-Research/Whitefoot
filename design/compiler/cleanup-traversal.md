Decision: Carry checked post-right-hand-side displacement liveness into the commit and derive any displaced release through the same type-based machinery as scope cleanup, because the same typed target may still hold a value, have been consumed by the right-hand side, or be a dead binding being reinitialized. Read a remaining old value after right-hand-side effects and release it after storing the replacement, instead of reconstructing source liveness in lowering or emitting empty release records for every scalar assignment.

Decision: Destructuring carries explicit field ordinals and covered-part releases in its checked statement, and an owned Box-content move carries its selected path and enclosing-owner cleanup separately from an ordinary content read, because skipped fields are not positional binders and whole-owner consumption must release residual affine parts and enclosing cells exactly once. Reuse the ordinary typed cleanup derivation for those residuals while withholding the moved result's release, instead of hidden binders whose cleanup waits until scope exit or a read-node flag every downstream reader must interpret. Linear residuals remain source errors and runtime-capacity contents remain unmovable under TYPE-9.

Decision: Semantic tail-transfer eligibility and backend cleanup emission read one cycle-safe type-release classification, with semantic and IR adapters supplying shapes to the same action and element-multiplicity rules, because an owner omitted by the checker but reclaimed by emitted cleanup invalidates the pre-transfer reference condition, instead of independent lists of releasing types.
