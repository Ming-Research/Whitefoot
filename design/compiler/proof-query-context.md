Decision: Reuse the ordinary L0 closure and ordered affine index across queries with the same entering facts and value images, and closed/type interval endpoints within one affine candidate traversal, rebuilding views when term or goal information changes, because the [query-cost study](https://github.com/mbbill/Whitefoot/blob/e84912f215072419093e66413b21e082dab36400/research/investigations/proof-certificate-architecture/CHECKING-COST.md#query-preparation-selection) isolates repeated preparation, instead of recomputing each premise or materializing new live facts for reuse. Candidate and diagnostic-parent traversal stay ordered within the prepared view; its L0 witness follows [incremental closure](https://github.com/mbbill/Whitefoot/blob/main/design/compiler/incremental-closure.md).

Decision: Ordinary affine queries and certificates share one function-local memo of the complete ordered L0 index, keyed by a retained closed-state identity and exact equality of the ordered candidate terms and value images after candidate formation, because the [stage-3 profile](../../research/investigations/check-time/DESIGN.md#profile-of-the-stage-3-interpreters-check) attributes 64% of checking-thread samples to rebuilding that index and the [reuse argument](../../research/investigations/check-time/DESIGN.md#direction-a2-reuse-the-complete-affine-index) covers changed facts, inventories and images without a second mutation protocol, instead of certificate-only reuse, pruning candidates by target overlap or incrementally repairing indexes across events. This is the Q155 prototype: a single memo bounds retained storage, every miss runs the existing full enumeration, and no timing improvement is yet established; reopen the representation if measurements show misses dominate or the differential fails.
