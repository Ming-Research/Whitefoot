Decision: A fact state records its completed ENT-4 closure, inserts new relations and fresh or killed terms' implicit bounds, and repairs cells weakened by candidate removal while both terms survive, because the [closure study](https://github.com/mbbill/Whitefoot/blob/b3341e32ab8d69e2974227aee7146ac16eb2fe00/research/investigations/proof-certificate-architecture/INCREMENTAL-CLOSURE.md#implementation) identifies repeated complete closure as the dominant cost, instead of recomputing it after every kill, join and query. More weakened cells than terms or unsettled repair use a seeded fixed point admitting only strictly smaller bounds and absent disequalities. A state without a recorded closure keeps its remembered closed view as a seed while it gains only bound candidates or signed goals, and its next closure inserts the bounds that became strictly smaller and every later term's implicit bounds into that view, because a flow state that has never been snapshotted otherwise recloses all of its terms at every proof as the function registers new terms, which the [compile-speed attribution](../../research/investigations/compile-speed/DESIGN.md#unrecorded-states) estimates as nearly all closure cost of the slowest real functions and whose removal took the slowest Snowghost module check from 41 s to 23 s, instead of the complete unseeded fixed point for every unrecorded state; any other unrecorded state, or a seed whose term table replaced a standing measure fact, uses the complete unseeded fixed point.

Decision: Equal L0 bounds may retain different valid derivations and ledger identities, because ENT-4 fixes derivability rather than witness choice, instead of forcing the complete traversal's witness. A join reuses a shared selected proof only when every non-contradictory predecessor contributes it and it is already independently live or rests on implicit bounds alone; temporary consequences still receive a join boundary. Ordinary fallback candidates survive removable postcondition dependencies, including delivery substitution and its join. A contradictory predecessor retains its proof but contributes no removable value dependency, because ENT-5 makes it absorbing and neutral. The [retained-proof argument](https://github.com/mbbill/Whitefoot/blob/b3341e32ab8d69e2974227aee7146ac16eb2fe00/research/investigations/proof-certificate-architecture/INCREMENTAL-CLOSURE.md#retained-derivations) explains why equal numeric values alone do not preserve later kills.

Decision: Selected bounds and closed views use dense matrices, with an unchanged state's closed view remembered, because the [representation attribution](https://github.com/mbbill/Whitefoot/blob/b3341e32ab8d69e2974227aee7146ac16eb2fe00/research/investigations/proof-certificate-architecture/INCREMENTAL-CLOSURE.md#implementation) identifies repeated map construction, key sorting and closure of unchanged states as remaining costs, instead of rebuilding hashed bounds at each closure, materialization and join.

Decision: A snapshot keeps a closed relation's proof unwrapped when that proof rests on [ENT-2] implicit bounds alone through [ENT-4] closure rules, and wraps every other relation in a materialized node at the snapshot event, while S11's preheader snapshot wraps every relation, because such a conclusion holds at every program point, so no kill or join can remove it and a snapshot boundary records nothing, while wrapping each of the nearly n-squared such cells at every snapshot dominated the slowest real functions in the [compile-speed attribution](../../research/investigations/compile-speed/DESIGN.md#implicit-only-snapshots), and S11's counted roots name the preheader snapshot as their proof point, instead of a materialized node for every closed cell at every snapshot.

Rejected:
- A second production difference-bound implementation with its own kill rules: rejected because term kills and candidate removals already have one flow implementation that records changed terms and cells; duplicating that rule would add an independent source of acceptance behavior. Test-only eager snapshots and joins advance their own states to check transition preservation, in addition to recomputing incremental closures from the optimized state's facts.
- Sharing matrix rows between the states that meet at a join: rejected because a kill or materialization in one arm rewrites a column in every row, so few rows would still be shared at the join.
- Storing only bounds stronger than the bound their terms' type ranges imply through zero: rejected because removing postcondition proofs at a kill, joining states and delivering relations to a receiver would each need a separate argument for the omitted cells, and no measurement shows that the remaining cost needs it.
