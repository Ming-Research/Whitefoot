Decision: A fact state records its completed ENT-4 closure, inserts new relations and fresh or killed terms' implicit bounds, and repairs cells weakened by candidate removal while both terms survive, because the [closure study](https://github.com/mbbill/Whitefoot/blob/b3341e32ab8d69e2974227aee7146ac16eb2fe00/research/investigations/proof-certificate-architecture/INCREMENTAL-CLOSURE.md#implementation) identifies repeated complete closure as the dominant cost, instead of recomputing it after every kill, join and query. More weakened cells than terms or unsettled repair use a seeded fixed point admitting only strictly smaller bounds and absent disequalities; no recorded closure or a missing term uses the complete unseeded fixed point.

Decision: Equal L0 bounds may retain different valid derivations and ledger identities, because ENT-4 fixes derivability rather than witness choice, instead of forcing the complete traversal's witness. A join reuses a shared selected proof only when every non-contradictory predecessor contributes it and it is already independently live or implicit; temporary consequences still receive a join boundary. Ordinary fallback candidates survive removable postcondition dependencies, including delivery substitution and its join. A contradictory predecessor retains its proof but contributes no removable value dependency, because ENT-5 makes it absorbing and neutral. The [retained-proof argument](https://github.com/mbbill/Whitefoot/blob/b3341e32ab8d69e2974227aee7146ac16eb2fe00/research/investigations/proof-certificate-architecture/INCREMENTAL-CLOSURE.md#retained-derivations) explains why equal numeric values alone do not preserve later kills.

Decision: Selected bounds and closed views use dense matrices over each state's active terms, the terms that hold a stored bound or disequality or an implicit fact against another non-Z term, with an unchanged state's closed view remembered, and a term outside that set is answered through Z from its two implicit bounds and the active term's Z cell, because the [representation attribution](https://github.com/mbbill/Whitefoot/blob/b3341e32ab8d69e2974227aee7146ac16eb2fe00/research/investigations/proof-certificate-architecture/INCREMENTAL-CLOSURE.md#implementation) identifies repeated map construction, key sorting and closure of unchanged states as remaining costs, and the [active-term measurement](../../research/investigations/proof-certificate-architecture/INCREMENTAL-CLOSURE.md#active-term-matrices) shows matrices over every registered term filling every cell with bounds composed through Z, so that a `match` of N arms whose terms the table never removes closed, materialized and joined a number of cells quadratic in N a number of times linear in N while at most thirteen terms carried a fact, instead of rebuilding hashed bounds at each closure, materialization and join or indexing the matrices by every registered term. A term holding only its reflexive and Z bounds cannot shorten a path between two other terms or be strengthened, so the closure over the active terms derives every bound the closure over all terms derives; a join stores a disequality on such a term only where the joined Z bounds no longer imply it, and the test oracle compares every registered pair of the active closure's view with the closure over all terms.

Rejected:
- A second production difference-bound implementation with its own kill rules: rejected because term kills and candidate removals already have one flow implementation that records changed terms and cells; duplicating that rule would add an independent source of acceptance behavior. Test-only eager snapshots and joins advance their own states to check transition preservation, in addition to recomputing incremental closures from the optimized state's facts.
- Sharing matrix rows between the states that meet at a join: rejected because a kill or materialization in one arm rewrites a column in every row, so few rows would still be shared at the join.
- Storing only bounds stronger than the bound their terms' type ranges imply through zero: rejected because removing postcondition proofs at a kill, joining states and delivering relations to a receiver would each need a separate argument for the omitted cells, and no measurement shows that the remaining cost needs it; the active-term matrices remove the measured cost while keeping every cell of two active terms, so the omitted cells are exactly those of a term with no fact beyond its Z bounds, and each consumer of those cells needs its own shorter argument: the view answers a query through Z, a join reads an input's missing row through that input's view and stores a disequality on an inactive term only where the joined Z bounds do not imply it, delivery enumerates the carrier's relations through the view, and the ordinary fallback of a materialization or join reads every pair of the canonical universe through the ordinary view; a kill drops a killed term's row, and a materialization before a kill on a closed record still closes for a goal contradiction.
