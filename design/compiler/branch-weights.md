Decision: Supply unlikely-edge LLVM branch weights as code-placement hints, separate from compiler/backend-facts' correctness promises, using Result failure arms and paths that all return a constructed Err (S1), paths in an actually split dispatch loop that all return without reaching its header (S2), and a two-way branch's call-containing side against a call-free continuation or return when the call is not unavoidable from the branch (S3), because the [call-path probe](../../research/investigations/halo-call-path/DESIGN.md#result-probe-run-38335991774) attributes the hot arms' avoidable spills to placement under unknown frequency, ERR-4 supplies failure classification, and splitting removes the host's loop-exit evidence, instead of changing calling conventions or making frequency a correctness assertion. This is proposed for E2: [E1](../../research/investigations/halo-call-path/DESIGN.md#result-e1-and-e3-run-38338456381) found no fib gain from error callees alone, while slow carried 59% of the combined gain and general string interning supplied most of the remainder; it did not show harm on concat or string-key, so S3 remains a measured hypothesis rather than a consequence of failure classification.

Decision: Use unlikely-to-likely weights of 1:2000 for S1 and S2 and a separately named 1:2000 ratio for S3, give structural predictions precedence, leave ties and ordinary loop branches without an S3 prediction, and estimate calls after inlining by excluding intrinsics, transfers, tail calls and bodies of at most sixteen IR instructions without a surviving ordinary call, because the [study's S3 proposal and E2 criteria](../../research/investigations/halo-call-path/DESIGN.md#approaches) require a general call-site estimate whose harm can be measured separately, instead of treating every pre-inlining helper as an out-of-line call. Small wrappers inherit their callees' estimated surviving calls; this size proxy is provisional, not a host-inlining guarantee. The temporary internal experiment setting selects S1+S2 as C1 or S1+S2+S3 as C2, defaulting to C2, and enters the emitted-module cache identity; the driver currently offers only its fixed optimizing level. Reopen the S3 ratio or proxy if E2's C2 harm bound of 1% more instructions than C1 on any Halo kernel or v2h CoreMark fails; the study owns the full performance selection criteria.

Rejected:
- preserve_most entries: rejected because the probe's convention-only variant saved eleven instructions in the three arms but added 57.5 instructions per Lua call to the whole fib workload; the clones prevented inlining and cost more than they saved.
- Callee-level cold as the primary mechanism: rejected because the useful expectation belongs to paths and call sites, while a general callee such as string interning also serves ordinary string construction; E1 found no harm on its two string kernels but does not establish coldness at every call site. Branch weights also preserve the ordinary inlining policy rather than shrinking every caller's budget.
- Profile-guided optimization as the default: rejected because it adds a training workload and profile to build inputs and prevents a like-for-like comparison with an unprofiled PUC Lua; E3's large, partly transferable gain is an experiment ceiling, not evidence that default builds should require training.
- A source frequency hint: rejected for this experiment because it states an unchecked expectation where current surface names label checked invariants; the study reserves an owner decision on that language change for failure of the structural call heuristic's harm criteria.
