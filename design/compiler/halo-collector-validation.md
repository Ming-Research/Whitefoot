Decision: Halo exposes opt-in safepoint collection stress and completed-collection observation through its embedding API, with new engines defaulting to stress off and the saturating count preserved across reset, because an embedding client needs a stated engine-lifecycle contract for forcing and observing the ordinary collector without depending on mutable heap telemetry storage, instead of having each research client configure and interpret public Heap fields directly.
